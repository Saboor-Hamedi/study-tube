# Writing Integrity & AI Detection

Writella features a professional-grade forensic suite designed to distinguish between human-written and machine-generated text.

### Neural Forensics
- **Local Engine**: GPT-2 Transformer (v6.2 Calibrated).
- **Privacy First**: All forensic audits are performed on-device (Port 8008).
- **Metrics**: Hybrid analysis of Perplexity, Burstiness, and **Top-K Profiling**.
- **Neural Scrub**: Automatic Markdown and noise removal for surgical accuracy.

### AI Detection Indicators
- **Top-K DNA**: Detects the 'predictable path' signature of modern LLMs.
- **Modern Model Calibration**: Specific heuristics for high-end models (Gemini, DeepSeek, GPT-4o).
- **Integrity Score**: Real-time percentage reflecting authorship probability.
- **Linguistic DNA**: Visual breakdown of forensic markers and anomaly mapping.
