# 🔴 Past Continuous Tense

---

## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **sixth tense** in the course.

👉 It builds on Past Simple.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to describe actions in progress in the past
- How to talk about interrupted actions
- How to use "was / were + verb + ing"
- The difference between Past Simple and Past Continuous

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

The Past Continuous is used when:
👉 An action was happening at a specific time in the past  
👉 The action was **in progress**

---

## 📖 Usage

### 1. Action in Progress in the Past

- I was studying at 8 PM
- She was reading

---

### 2. Interrupted Action

- I was studying when he called
- She was sleeping when the phone rang

👉 Long action (continuous) + short action (past simple)

---

### 3. Background Description

- It was raining
- People were talking

---

## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + was / were + verb + ing

---

### Examples

- I was working
- You were studying
- They were playing

- He was working
- She was reading
- It was raining

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Use correct form

- I / He / She / It → was
- You / We / They → were

---

### 2. Use verb + ing

- work → working
- read → reading

---

## ❌ Negative Form

- I was not working
- You were not studying

👉 Short form:

- wasn’t
- weren’t

---

### Examples

- He wasn’t working
- They weren’t playing

---

## ❓ Questions

- Was I working?
- Were you studying?

- Was she reading?
- Were they playing?

---

## 🔄 Past Simple vs Past Continuous

### Past Simple

- I studied yesterday

👉 Completed action

---

### Past Continuous

- I was studying at 8 PM

👉 Action in progress

---

### Combined Example

- I was studying when he called

👉 Background + interruption

---

## ✍️ Writing Practice

---

### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I was studying
- I was working
- I was reading

---

### Step 2 – Add Time

- I was studying at 8 PM
- I was working yesterday evening

---

### Step 3 – Personalize

- I was studying English last night
- I was working on my project

---

### Step 4 – Expand

- I was studying English last night when my friend called

---

### Step 5 – Combine

- I was studying English, and my brother was watching TV

---

---

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I was study  
✅ I was studying

❌ They was playing  
✅ They were playing

❌ I studied when he was calling  
✅ I was studying when he called

---

## 🏁 Lesson Summary

- Used for actions in progress in the past
- Structure: was/were + verb + ing
- Often used with Past Simple
