# 🔴 Past Perfect Continuous

---

## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **eighth tense** in the course.

👉 It completes the **Past Tense system**.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to describe actions that continued before a past event
- How to express duration in the past
- How to use "had been + verb + ing"
- The difference between Past Perfect and Past Perfect Continuous

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

The Past Perfect Continuous is used when:
👉 An action started in the past  
👉 Continued for some time  
👉 Happened **before another past action**

---

## 📖 Usage

### 1. Duration Before a Past Event

- I had been studying for 3 hours before the exam started
- She had been working all day before she rested

---

### 2. Showing Cause in the Past

- I was tired because I had been working
- He was wet because he had been walking in the rain

---

## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + had been + verb + ing

---

### Examples

- I had been working
- You had been studying
- They had been playing

- He had been working
- She had been reading
- It had been raining

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Same for all subjects

👉 Always use:

- had been

---

### 2. Use verb + ing

- work → working
- study → studying

---

## ⏱ Time Expressions

Very common:

- for (duration) → for 2 hours
- since (starting point) → since morning

---

### Examples

- I had been studying for 2 hours before the test
- She had been working since morning before she left

---

## ❌ Negative Form

- I had not been working
- You hadn’t been studying

- He had not been reading
- She hadn’t been playing

---

## ❓ Questions

- Had you been studying?
- Had they been working?

- Had she been reading?
- Had he been playing?

---

## 🔄 Past Perfect vs Continuous

### Past Perfect

- I had finished my work

👉 Focus = result

---

### Past Perfect Continuous

- I had been working for 3 hours

👉 Focus = duration

---

## ✍️ Writing Practice

---

### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I had been studying
- I had been working
- I had been reading

---

### Step 2 – Add Time

- I had been studying for 2 hours
- I had been working all day

---

### Step 3 – Add Second Action

- I had been studying for 2 hours before the exam started

---

### Step 4 – Personalize

- I had been working on my project for days before I finished it

---

### Step 5 – Combine

- I had been studying for hours before the exam started, and I felt tired

---

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I had been study  
✅ I had been studying

❌ She had been work  
✅ She had been working

❌ I had worked for 2 hours before the exam (wrong meaning)  
✅ I had been working for 2 hours before the exam

## 🏁 Lesson Summary

- Action continued before another past action
- Structure: had been + verb + ing
