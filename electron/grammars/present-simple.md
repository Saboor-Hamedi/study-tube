# 🔵 Present Simple Tense

## 🚀 Overview

The Present Simple is the **most important tense in English**.

You use it to talk about:

- Daily routines
- Facts
- general truths
- repeated actions

👉 This tense covers a huge part of everyday communication.

---

## 🎯 When to Use

### 1. Daily Habits

- I wake up at 7 AM
- She drinks coffee every morning

---

### 2. General Facts

- The sun rises in the east
- Water boils at 100°C

---

### 3. Repeated Actions

- I go to the gym every day
- They play football on weekends

---

## 🧱 Structure

### ✅ Basic Form

- I / You / We / They → base verb
- He / She / It → verb + **s / es**

---

### 💡 Examples

- I work
- You study
- They play

👉 With "he/she/it":

- He works
- She studies
- It rains

---

## ⚠️ Important Rule (Very Important)

👉 Add **-s / -es** with:

- He
- She
- It

❌ She work  
✅ She works

---

## 🔤 Spelling Rules

### Add **-s**

- work → works
- play → plays

---

### Add **-es** (verbs ending in s, sh, ch, x, o)

- go → goes
- watch → watches

---

### Change **y → ies**

- study → studies
- try → tries

---

## ❌ Negative Sentences

### Structure:

- do not (don’t)
- does not (doesn’t)

---

### Examples:

- I do not work
- You don’t study

- He does not work
- She doesn’t study

👉 After "does", use base verb:

❌ She doesn’t works  
✅ She doesn’t work

---

## ❓ Questions

### Structure:

- Do / Does + subject + base verb

---

### Examples:

- Do you work?
- Do they study?

- Does she work?
- Does he play?

---

## 💬 Short Answers

- Do you work? → Yes, I do / No, I don’t
- Does she work? → Yes, she does / No, she doesn’t

# ✍️ Writing Practice

---

## 🧩 Step 1 – Controlled Writing

Write simple sentences:

- I eat rice
- I study English
- I wake up early

---

## 🔄 Step 2 – Change Words

- I eat rice → I eat bread
- I study English → I study programming

---

## 🧠 Step 3 – Personalize

Write about your life:

- I wake up at 6 AM
- I study coding every day
- I drink coffee in the morning

---

## 🧪 Step 4 – Expand

- I study coding →  
  I study coding every day to improve my skills

---

## 🔗 Step 5 – Combine

- I study coding every day, and I practice building apps

---

## 📈 Daily Practice Task

Write **5 sentences** about your daily routine:

Example:

- I wake up at 7 AM
- I check my phone
- I study English
- I work on my project
- I sleep at 11 PM

---

## 🎯 Challenge

Write a short paragraph:

👉 "My Daily Routine"

(3–5 sentences)

---

## ⚡ Pro Tips

- Don’t forget **-s with he/she/it**
- Keep sentences simple
- Focus on repetition

---

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ She go to school  
✅ She goes to school

❌ He don’t like coffee  
✅ He doesn’t like coffee

---

## 🏁 Summary

- Use for habits, facts, routines
- Add **-s** for he/she/it
- Use **do/does** for questions and negatives
