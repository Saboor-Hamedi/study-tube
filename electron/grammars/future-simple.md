# 🟢 Future Simple (Will)

---

## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **ninth tense** in the course.

👉 It starts the **Future Tense system**.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to talk about future decisions
- How to make predictions
- How to use "will + base verb"
- When to use "will" vs other future forms

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

The Future Simple is used when:
👉 You decide something **at the moment of speaking**  
👉 You make a prediction about the future

---

## 📖 Usage

### 1. Instant Decisions

- I will call you
- I will help you

👉 Decision made now

---

### 2. Predictions

- It will rain tomorrow
- She will succeed

---

### 3. Promises / Offers

- I will help you
- I will be there

---

## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + will + base verb

---

### Examples

- I will work
- You will study
- They will play

- He will work
- She will study
- It will rain

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Same for all subjects

👉 No change:

- I / You / He / She / They → **will**

---

### 2. Use base verb

- go → go
- eat → eat

❌ will goes  
✅ will go

---

## ❌ Negative Form

- I will not work
- You won’t study

- He will not go
- She won’t eat

---

## ❓ Questions

- Will you work?
- Will they study?

- Will she go?
- Will he eat?

---

## 💬 Short Answers

- Will you come? → Yes, I will / No, I won’t
- Will she help? → Yes, she will / No, she won’t

---

## 🔄 Will vs Going To

### Will

- I will study

👉 Decision now

---

### Going to

- I am going to study

👉 Plan already decided

---

## ✍️ Writing Practice

---

### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I will study
- I will work
- I will learn

---

### Step 2 – Add Details

- I will study English
- I will work tomorrow

---

### Step 3 – Personalize

- I will study English tonight
- I will work on my project

---

### Step 4 – Expand

- I will study English tonight because I have free time

---

### Step 5 – Combine

- I will study English tonight, and I will watch a video

---

## 📈 Task

Write 5 sentences about your future:

Example:

- I will study English

---

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I will goes  
✅ I will go

❌ She will studies  
✅ She will study

❌ I will to go  
✅ I will go

---
