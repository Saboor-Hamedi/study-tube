# English Tenses – Complete Guide

## 🧠 Overview

English has **3 main time frames**:

- Present
- Past
- Future

Each has **4 aspects**:

- Simple
- Continuous (Progressive)
- Perfect
- Perfect Continuous

👉 Total = **12 tenses**

---

# 🔵 PRESENT TENSES

---

## 1. Present Simple

### ✅ Use

- Facts → The sun rises in the east.
- Habits → I study every day.
- General truths → Water boils at 100°C.

### 🧱 Structure

- I/You/We/They + base verb
- He/She/It + verb + **s/es**

### 💡 Examples

- I work daily.
- She works daily.

---

## 2. Present Continuous

### ✅ Use

- Actions happening now → I am studying.
- Temporary actions → She is working today.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + am/is/are + verb + ing

### 💡 Examples

- I am reading.
- They are playing.

---

## 3. Present Perfect

### ✅ Use

- Experience → I have visited Paris.
- Result now → She has finished her work.
- Unspecified time → I have seen that movie.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + have/has + past participle (V3)

### 💡 Examples

- I have eaten.
- He has gone home.

---

## 4. Present Perfect Continuous

### ✅ Use

- Action started in past and still continues  
  → I have been studying for 2 hours.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + have/has been + verb + ing

### 💡 Examples

- She has been working all day.
- They have been waiting.

---

# 🔴 PAST TENSES

---

## 5. Past Simple

### ✅ Use

- Completed actions in the past  
  → I visited London yesterday.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + verb (V2)

### 💡 Examples

- I ate rice.
- She worked hard.

---

## 6. Past Continuous

### ✅ Use

- Action in progress in the past  
  → I was studying at 8 PM.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + was/were + verb + ing

### 💡 Examples

- I was reading.
- They were playing.

---

## 7. Past Perfect

### ✅ Use

- One action finished before another  
  → I had finished before he arrived.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + had + past participle

### 💡 Examples

- She had left.
- We had eaten.

---

## 8. Past Perfect Continuous

### ✅ Use

- Duration before a past event  
  → I had been studying for 3 hours before the exam.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + had been + verb + ing

### 💡 Examples

- He had been working all day.
- They had been waiting.

---

# 🟢 FUTURE TENSES

---

## 9. Future Simple

### ✅ Use

- Decisions → I will call you.
- Predictions → It will rain.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + will + base verb

### 💡 Examples

- I will go.
- She will help.

---

## 10. “Going To” Future

### ✅ Use

- Plans → I am going to study.
- Evidence → Look! It is going to rain.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb

### 💡 Examples

- I am going to travel.
- They are going to eat.

---

## 11. Future Continuous

### ✅ Use

- Action in progress at a future time  
  → I will be studying at 8 PM.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + will be + verb + ing

### 💡 Examples

- I will be working.
- She will be sleeping.

---

## 12. Future Perfect

### ✅ Use

- Action completed before a future time  
  → I will have finished by tomorrow.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + will have + past participle

### 💡 Examples

- I will have done it.
- They will have arrived.

---

## 13. Future Perfect Continuous

### ✅ Use

- Duration until a future point  
  → I will have been studying for 5 hours by 10 PM.

### 🧱 Structure

- Subject + will have been + verb + ing

### 💡 Examples

- She will have been working.
- We will have been waiting.
