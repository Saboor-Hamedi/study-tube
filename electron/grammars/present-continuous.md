# 🔵 Present Continuous Tense


## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **second tense** in the course.

👉 It builds directly on Present Simple.


## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to talk about actions happening now
- How to describe temporary situations
- How to form sentences using "am / is / are"
- The difference between Present Simple and Present Continuous


## 🧠 Core Idea

The Present Continuous is used for actions that are:

- Happening right now
- Temporary
- In progress


## 📖 Usage

### 1. Actions Happening Now

- I am studying
- She is reading


### 2. Temporary Actions

- I am working from home today
- They are staying in a hotel


### 3. Changing Situations

- The weather is getting colder
- My English is improving


## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + am / is / are + verb + ing


### Examples

- I am working
- You are studying
- They are playing

- He is working
- She is reading
- It is raining


## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Use correct "be" verb

- I → am
- He/She/It → is
- You/We/They → are


### 2. Add **-ing** to the verb

- work → working
- read → reading


## 🔤 Spelling Rules

### Add -ing

- play → playing


### Drop final "e"

- make → making
- write → writing


### Double consonant (short verbs)

- run → running
- sit → sitting


## ❌ Negative Form

- I am not working
- You are not studying

- He is not working
- She is not reading

👉 Short form:

- I’m not
- He isn’t
- They aren’t


## ❓ Questions

- Am I working?
- Are you studying?

- Is she working?
- Is he playing?


## 🔄 Present Simple vs Continuous

### Present Simple

- I work every day

### Present Continuous

- I am working right now

👉 Simple = routine  
👉 Continuous = now


## ✍️ Writing Practice


### Step 1 – Basic Sentences

- I am studying
- I am reading
- I am learning English


### Step 2 – Add Details

- I am studying English
- I am reading a book


### Step 3 – Personalize

- I am studying English at home
- I am working on my project


### Step 4 – Expand

- I am studying English at home because I want to improve


### Step 5 – Combine

- I am studying English, and I am listening to a podcast

Example:

- I am sitting

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I am work  
✅ I am working

❌ She is go  
✅ She is going

❌ I working  
✅ I am working


## 🏁 Lesson Summary

- Use for actions happening now
- Structure: am/is/are + verb + ing
- Not used for habits
