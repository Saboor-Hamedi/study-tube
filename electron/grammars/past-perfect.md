# 🔴 Past Perfect Tense

---

## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **seventh tense** in the course.

👉 It adds depth to past storytelling.

---

## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to describe actions that happened before another past action
- How to use "had + past participle"
- How to clearly show the order of past events
- The difference between Past Simple and Past Perfect

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

The Past Perfect is used when:
👉 One action happened **before another action in the past**

---

## 📖 Usage

### 1. Earlier Past Action

- I had finished my work before he arrived
- She had left before I came

---

### 2. Showing Order of Events

- I had eaten before I went out
- They had completed the project before the deadline

---

👉 First action = Past Perfect  
👉 Second action = Past Simple

---

## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + had + past participle (V3)

---

### Examples

- I had finished
- You had studied
- They had played

- He had gone
- She had eaten
- It had stopped

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Same for all subjects

👉 No change:

- I / You / He / She / They → **had**

---

### 2. Use past participle (V3)

- go → gone
- eat → eaten
- see → seen

---

## ❌ Negative Form

- I had not finished
- You hadn’t studied

- He had not gone
- She hadn’t eaten

---

## ❓ Questions

- Had you finished?
- Had they studied?

- Had she gone?
- Had he eaten?

---

## 🔄 Past Simple vs Past Perfect

### Past Simple

- I ate before I left

👉 Order is not very clear

---

### Past Perfect

- I had eaten before I left

👉 Order is very clear

---

## ⏱ Common Words

- before
- after
- already
- by the time

---

### Examples

- I had finished before he arrived
- She had already left
- By the time I arrived, they had gone

---

## ✍️ Writing Practice

---

### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I had finished
- I had studied
- I had eaten

---

### Step 2 – Add Second Action

- I had finished before he came
- I had studied before the exam

---

### Step 3 – Personalize

- I had completed my project before the deadline
- I had studied English before I moved

---

### Step 4 – Expand

- I had studied English before I moved, so I felt confident

---

### Step 5 – Combine

- I had finished my work before he arrived, and I went home

---

---

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I had went  
✅ I had gone

❌ She had ate  
✅ She had eaten

❌ I finished before he had come  
✅ I had finished before he came
