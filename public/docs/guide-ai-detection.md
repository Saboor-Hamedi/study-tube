# 🛡️ Writing Integrity & AI Detection

Writella features a professional-grade forensic suite designed to distinguish between human-written and machine-generated text.

### Neural Forensics
- **Local Engine**: Utilizes a GPT-2 transformer engine for local inference.
- **Privacy First**: All calculations are performed on-device to ensure data sovereignty.
- **Metrics**: Detailed analysis of Perplexity and Burstiness.

### AI Detection Indicators
- **Integrity Score**: A high-fidelity percentage reflecting the linguistic probability of human authorship.
- **Linguistic Fingerprinting**: Patterns in sentence structure and variation are analyzed to identify machine artifacts.
