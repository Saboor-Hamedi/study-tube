# 🔍 Plagiarism & Citation Audit

The Plagiarism suite ensures academic rigor by verifying the originality of manuscripts.

### Analytical Pillars
- **Source Verification**: Cross-referencing text against global research databases.
- **Citation Integrity**: Ensuring that references are correctly formatted and attributed.
- **Zero-Tolerance Audit**: Surgical scanning for verbatim or paraphrased content without proper credit.
