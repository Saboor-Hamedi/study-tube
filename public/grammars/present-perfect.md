# 🔵 Present Perfect Tense


## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **third tense** in the course.

👉 It connects the **past and the present**.


## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to talk about experiences
- How to describe completed actions with present results
- How to use "have / has + past participle"
- The difference between past and present perfect


## 🧠 Core Idea

The Present Perfect is used when:
👉 The time is **not important**  
👉 The result is important **now**


## 📖 Usage

### 1. Life Experience

- I have visited Bali
- She has tried sushi

👉 (We don’t say when)


### 2. Result Now

- I have finished my work
- She has cleaned the room

👉 (The result is visible now)


### 3. Unfinished Time

- I have studied today
- He has worked this week


## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + have / has + past participle (V3)


### Examples

- I have eaten
- You have studied
- They have played

- He has eaten
- She has studied
- It has stopped


## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Use correct helper verb

- I / You / We / They → have
- He / She / It → has


### 2. Use past participle (V3)

- eat → eaten
- go → gone
- see → seen


## ❌ Negative Form

- I have not eaten
- You haven’t studied

- He has not gone
- She hasn’t finished


## ❓ Questions

- Have you eaten?
- Have they finished?

- Has she gone?
- Has he studied?


## 🔄 Past Simple vs Present Perfect

### Past Simple

- I ate yesterday

👉 Time is clear


### Present Perfect

- I have eaten

👉 Time is not important


## ⏱ Common Time Expressions

- ever
- never
- already
- yet
- just


### Examples

- I have already finished
- She has just arrived
- Have you eaten yet?
- I have never tried that


## ✍️ Writing Practice


### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I have eaten
- I have studied
- I have finished


### Step 2 – Add Words

- I have eaten lunch
- I have studied English


### Step 3 – Personalize

- I have studied English for two years
- I have finished my project


### Step 4 – Expand

- I have studied English for two years, and I feel more confident


### Step 5 – Combine

- I have finished my work, and I have started a new project

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I have went  
✅ I have gone

❌ She have finished  
✅ She has finished

❌ I have seen yesterday  
✅ I saw yesterday


## 🏁 Lesson Summary

- Use for experience and results
- Structure: have/has + V3
- Time is not specific
