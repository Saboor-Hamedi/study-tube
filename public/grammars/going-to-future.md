# 🟢 Going To (Future Plans)


## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **tenth lesson** in the course.

👉 It builds on Future Simple (will).


## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to talk about future plans
- How to express intentions
- How to use "am / is / are + going to"
- The difference between "will" and "going to"


## 🧠 Core Idea

"Going to" is used when:
👉 The plan is already decided  
👉 There is evidence something will happen


## 📖 Usage

### 1. Future Plans

- I am going to study tonight
- She is going to travel next week

👉 Decision made before speaking


### 2. Intentions

- I am going to learn English
- They are going to build a project


### 3. Evidence-Based Predictions

- Look at the sky! It is going to rain
- He is going to fall

👉 You can see it happening


## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + am / is / are + going to + base verb


### Examples

- I am going to study
- You are going to work
- They are going to play

- He is going to study
- She is going to work
- It is going to rain


## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Use correct "be" verb

- I → am
- He / She / It → is
- You / We / They → are


### 2. Use base verb

❌ going to studies  
✅ going to study


## ❌ Negative Form

- I am not going to study
- You are not going to work

- He is not going to go
- She is not going to eat

👉 Short form:

- I’m not
- He isn’t
- They aren’t


## ❓ Questions

- Am I going to study?
- Are you going to work?

- Is she going to travel?
- Is he going to eat?


## 🔄 Will vs Going To

### Will

- I will study

👉 Decision now


### Going to

- I am going to study

👉 Plan already decided


### Example Comparison

- I will call him (decision now)
- I am going to call him (plan already made)


## ✍️ Writing Practice


### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I am going to study
- I am going to work
- I am going to learn


### Step 2 – Add Details

- I am going to study English
- I am going to work tomorrow


### Step 3 – Personalize

- I am going to study English tonight
- I am going to build a project


### Step 4 – Expand

- I am going to study English tonight because I want to improve


### Step 5 – Combine

- I am going to study English tonight, and I am going to watch a lesson


## 📈 Task

Write 5 sentences about your plans:

Example:

- I am going to study English
- I am going to build an app
- I am going to learn new skills
- I am going to practice daily
- I am going to improve my future


## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I going to study  
✅ I am going to study

❌ She going to work  
✅ She is going to work

❌ I am going to studies  
✅ I am going to study


## 🏁 Lesson Summary

- Used for plans and intentions
- Structure: am/is/are + going to + verb
- Different from "will" (instant decisions)
