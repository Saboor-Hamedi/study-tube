# 🔴 Past Simple Tense


## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **fifth tense** in the course.

👉 It starts the **Past Tense system**.


## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to talk about completed actions in the past
- How to use past verbs (V2)
- How to form negative sentences and questions
- How to use time expressions correctly


## 🧠 Core Idea

The Past Simple is used when:
👉 An action started and finished in the past  
👉 The time is usually **clear or known**


## 📖 Usage

### 1. Completed Actions

- I visited Bali
- She finished her work


### 2. Specific Time in the Past

- I studied yesterday
- They played last night


### 3. Sequence of Events

- I woke up, ate breakfast, and went to work


## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + verb (V2)


### Examples

- I worked
- You studied
- They played

- He worked
- She studied
- It rained


## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Regular Verbs (add -ed)

- work → worked
- play → played


### 2. Irregular Verbs (change form)

- go → went
- eat → ate
- see → saw

👉 These must be memorized over time


## ❌ Negative Form

### Structure:

- did not (didn’t) + base verb


### Examples:

- I did not work
- You didn’t study

- He did not go
- She didn’t eat

👉 Always use base verb after "did"


## ❓ Questions

### Structure:

- Did + subject + base verb


### Examples:

- Did you work?
- Did they study?

- Did she go?
- Did he eat?


## 💬 Short Answers

- Did you work? → Yes, I did / No, I didn’t
- Did she go? → Yes, she did / No, she didn’t


## ⏱ Time Expressions

Common words used with Past Simple:

- yesterday
- last night / week / year
- ago
- in 2020


### Examples

- I studied yesterday
- She worked last week
- They moved two years ago


## ✍️ Writing Practice


### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I worked
- I studied
- I played


### Step 2 – Add Time

- I worked yesterday
- I studied last night


### Step 3 – Personalize

- I studied English yesterday
- I worked on my project last week


### Step 4 – Expand

- I studied English yesterday because I had free time


### Step 5 – Combine

- I studied English yesterday, and I watched a movie

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I did not went  
✅ I did not go

❌ She go yesterday  
✅ She went yesterday

❌ Did you went?  
✅ Did you go?


## 🏁 Lesson Summary

- Used for completed past actions
- Use V2 (past form)
- Use "did" for negatives and questions
