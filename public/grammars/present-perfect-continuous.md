# 🔵 Present Perfect Continuous


## 📌 Position in Course

This is the **fourth tense** in the course.

👉 It completes the **Present Tense system**.


## 🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson, you will learn:

- How to describe actions that started in the past and continue now
- How to express duration (how long)
- How to use "have been + verb + ing"
- The difference between Present Perfect and Present Perfect Continuous


## 🧠 Core Idea

The Present Perfect Continuous is used when:
👉 An action started in the past  
👉 It is still happening now  
👉 We focus on **duration**


## 📖 Usage

### 1. Actions Continuing Until Now

- I have been studying for 2 hours
- She has been working all day


### 2. Emphasizing Duration

- They have been waiting for a long time
- He has been learning English since 2020


### 3. Recently Stopped (Visible Effect)

- I am tired because I have been running
- She is wet because she has been walking in the rain


## 🧱 Structure

### Basic Form

- Subject + have / has been + verb + ing


### Examples

- I have been working
- You have been studying
- They have been playing

- He has been working
- She has been reading
- It has been raining


## ⚠️ Important Rules

### 1. Use correct helper

- I / You / We / They → have been
- He / She / It → has been


### 2. Use verb + ing

- study → studying
- work → working


## ⏱ Time Expressions

👉 These are very common:

- for (duration) → for 2 hours
- since (starting point) → since morning


### Examples

- I have been studying for 3 hours
- She has been working since morning


## ❌ Negative Form

- I have not been working
- You haven’t been studying

- He has not been working
- She hasn’t been reading


## ❓ Questions

- Have you been studying?
- Have they been working?

- Has she been reading?
- Has he been playing?


## 🔄 Present Perfect vs Continuous

### Present Perfect

- I have studied English

👉 Focus = result


### Present Perfect Continuous

- I have been studying English for 2 hours

👉 Focus = duration


## ✍️ Writing Practice


### Step 1 – Simple Sentences

- I have been studying
- I have been working
- I have been learning


### Step 2 – Add Time

- I have been studying for 2 hours
- I have been working all day


### Step 3 – Personalize

- I have been learning English for one year
- I have been working on my project


### Step 4 – Expand

- I have been learning English for one year because I want to improve


### Step 5 – Combine

- I have been studying English for two hours, and I feel tired

## 🚫 Common Mistakes

❌ I have been study  
✅ I have been studying

❌ She has been work  
✅ She has been working

❌ I am studying for 2 hours (wrong meaning)  
✅ I have been studying for 2 hours
