# Neural Feedback Hub: Industrial Roadmap (Phase 2)

This document outlines the next generation of academic forensic and synthesis features proposed for the **Neural Feedback Hub**. The focus is on **Industrial Accuracy, Version Control, and Seamless Research Synthesis**.

---

## 🧠 1. Forensic Engine Upgrades ("The Grammarly Fix")
Evolving the basic pattern-matching into a professional-grade linguistic diagnostic suite.

- **Objective**: Move beyond regex toward context-aware forensic analysis.
- **Key Enhancements**:
  - **Contextual Semantic Analysis**: Detecting errors that are spelled correctly but used wrongly (e.g., *affect* vs. *effect*).
  - **Academic Rigor Scoring**: A specific diagnostic that flags "Hedge Words" (e.g., *probably*, *maybe*) and replaces them with assertive academic phrasing.
  - **Logical Flow Mapping**: Identifying "Run-on Sentences" or "Comma Splices" that disrupt formal rhythm.
  - **Citation Forensic**: A sub-system to scan and validate academic citation formats (APA/MLA/Chicago).

## ⏳ 2. Neural Time-Travel (Versioning)
Implementing a professional-grade drafting history and diffing system.

- **Objective**: Track the evolution of academic drafts with precise delta-tracking.
- **Key Enhancements**:
  - **Version Diff Engine**: A side-by-side "Compare" mode showing color-coded deletions and additions between Draft 1, 2, and 3.
  - **Rollback Protocol**: The ability to revert specific linguistic corrections or restore previous versions of a paragraph.
  - **Timeline Navigation**: A visual scrub-bar for the Forge to navigate through drafting milestones.

## 🔗 3. Synthesis & "Drag-to-Forge"
Bridging the gap between active research fragments and the drafting pipeline.

- **Objective**: Enable fluid movement of knowledge from the Library into the Forge.
- **Key Enhancements**:
  - **Insight Injection**: Dragging research cards directly into the Forge text area with automatic academic formatting and citation.
  - **Multi-Source Merging**: A "Synthesis" tool that takes multiple selected library cards and generates a "Literature Review" draft.
  - **Contextual Anchoring**: Linking specific sentences in the Forge back to the original source video or research card in the Library.

## ✨ 4. Advanced UI Micro-Interactions
Elevating the user experience with high-fidelity "Academic Dashboard" aesthetics.

- **Objective**: Create a responsive, alive interface that provides real-time feedback.
- **Key Enhancements**:
  - **Laser-Scan Animation**: A horizontal "Neural Beam" that traverses the text area during deep analysis.
  - **Glassmorphic Tooltips**: Instant pop-over explanations for highlights that show "Reasoning" and "Correction" without checking the sidebar.
  - **Dynamic HUD**: Real-time "Academic Score" speedometers in the footer that update as the user types.

## 📤 5. The Industrial Hand-off
Professionalizing the output and collaboration pipeline.

- **Objective**: Streamlining the transition from "Draft" to "Published Work."
- **Key Enhancements**:
  - **Student Audit PDF**: Generating formal grading documents that embed the Feedback Hub diagnostics alongside the draft.
  - **Batch Analysis**: Bulk-scanning multiple student submissions to identify recurring error patterns across a class.
  - **Submission Pipeline**: Integrating the "Send to Student" button with a secure feedback loop and annotation tracking.

---

*This roadmap is a living document intended to guide the industrial expansion of the Neural Research Studio.*
